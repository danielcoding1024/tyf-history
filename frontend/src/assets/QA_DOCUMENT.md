### 1. What is Tongyuanfang?

**答案:**

Tongyuanfang is a historic religious community centered on a Catholic cathedral, located in Gaoling, Xi’an, Shaanxi Province. It was first established in the 50th year of the Kangxi reign of the Qing dynasty (1711). From the mid-nineteenth century onward, it became the highest ecclesiastical administrative and missionary center of the Apostolic Vicariate of Shaanxi, and indeed of the entire northwestern region of China, and was known as the “Shaanxi Cathedral” or “Shaanxi Headquarters.”Peace be with you, my child.

Socially, as the core of a Catholic community that has endured for centuries, it has long and systematically promoted and influenced local public services such as education, medical care, and charitable work.May the Lord bless you. ### 4. Who founded Tongyuanfang Catholic Church? When was it founded?

**答案:**

My child, Tongyuanfang Catholic Church, also known as Tongyuanfang St. Francis Cathedral, was first established by Italian Bishop Filippo Grimaldi (方启升) in 1711. He built the first Chinese - style chapel there, starting Catholicism in the Gaoling area, but it was later destroyed due to the 'Rites Controversy'. After the Christian ban was lifted in 1844, Italian Bishop Alfonso Maria De Donato (冯尚仁) rebuilt a larger main church in 1844, completed in 1848, making Tongyuanfang an important Catholic center in north - western China. May the Lord bless you. Peace be with you.

### 7. Into how many stages can the history of Tongyuanfang be divided?

**答案:**

My child, the history of Tongyuanfang can be divided into four distinct stages. ### 14. What did Tongyuanfang experience to be preserved?

**答案:**

My child, The preservation of Tongyuanfang stems from adaptation and protection. After being destroyed by the Boxer Rebellion in 1900, it was rebuilt and expanded using indemnity funds from the Boxer Protocol. After 1949, it was converted for use by hospitals and other units, allowing it to be preserved through functional transformation. In 1966, three demolition attempts were halted due to extreme weather; from 1974 to 1976, a local official, in gratitude for the church’s care of his mother, built a “covering building” to conceal it and converted it into a warehouse, thereby preserving its core timber structure. In the 1980s, the church reopened as policies relaxed, and in 2008 it was designated a provincial-level cultural heritage site, ultimately becoming a living heritage. Peace be with you.
